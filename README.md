## 🔧 Troubleshooting (If Something Goes Wrong)

### "Application won't start"
- **Restart your computer** – Sometimes a simple reboot fixes everything.
- **Update your GPU drivers** – Go to NVIDIA's website and download the latest driver for your graphics card.
- **Reinstall the app** – Uninstall it, restart, then install it again.

### "It's running slowly"
- **Close other programs** – Especially web browsers with many tabs.
- **Check your GPU memory** – In the Settings menu, lower the "Context Window" setting (e.g., from 32K to 16K).
- **Restart the app** – This clears temporary memory.

### "I get an error about missing files"
- **Run as administrator** – Right-click the app icon, then choose "Run as administrator."
- **Check your firewall** – Make sure your antivirus isn't blocking the app. Add it to the "allow list."

### "The AI is giving short answers"
- **Type more detailed prompts** – For example, instead of "Summarize," type "Please provide a detailed 3-paragraph summary."
- **Increase response length** – In Settings, adjust "Max Tokens" to a higher number (e.g., 2048).
